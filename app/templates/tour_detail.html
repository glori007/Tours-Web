Overview

* Don't forget to check out and book our other tours in the "New Town" and the "Prague Castle."

Want to start your holiday in Prague well? Join our 2-in-1 tour. The tour that actually includes 2 tours. Discover the Old Town, Lesser Town, Jewish Quarter and Charles Bridge with us!

When you talk about the Old Town, you are talking about the old historical part of Prague. There was little bombing here during the Second World War, so almost all buildings are still standing and in their original condition. Prague's Old Town is truly an impressive open-air museum. During the tour, you will explore exciting places such as Old Town Square, the Astronomical Clock, Charles Bridge, and the Jewish Quarter.

This walking tour is easy for any company or family to keep up with. On our tour, you will see all the main must-see sights.

Tour Stops

  1. Old Town SquareThe beating heart of Prague, surrounded by Gothic, Baroque and Renaissance architecture.
  2. Astronomical ClockOne of the most visited sights in Europe, with stories behind the hourly procession and its 600-year history.
  3. Church of Our Lady before TynThe iconic twin Gothic towers that define the Prague skyline.
  4. Charles BridgeCross the Vltava with statues, river views, and legends from Prague's medieval life.
  5. Jewish QuarterLearn about synagogues, old lanes, and the history of Josefov.
  6. Powder Gate & Republic SquareFinish near the Royal Route with recommendations for what to explore next.
  7. Municipal HouseSee one of Prague's finest Art Nouveau buildings and hear how it connects to Czech independence.
  8. Republic Square Wrap-upEnd with local recommendations for food, viewpoints, and nearby places to visit after the tour.
