FAQs about free tours in Prague

  • Are free walking tours in Prague really free?

    Yes. At the end of the tour, you simply pay whatever amount you feel it was worth.

  • Do I need to book a walking tour in advance?

    Yes. Booking in advance is required because tours can fill quickly.

  • How much should I tip on a free tour in Prague?

    Amounts vary based on tour length, guide quality, group size, and personal budget.

  • Are walking tours suitable for kids?

    Yes. Check the tour description to decide whether your children can handle the route.

  • What languages are Prague walking tours in?

    Most tours are available in English, with selected options in Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, and French.
